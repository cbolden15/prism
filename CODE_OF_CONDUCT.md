# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Prism contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make
participation respectful and free from harassment. Be direct about technical
disagreements, assume good faith until evidence says otherwise, and focus
criticism on the work rather than the person.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting personal boundaries and different levels of experience;
- keeping private data and security reports out of public threads.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, or
retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Prism project. It
also applies to private project communication when that conduct affects a
person's ability to participate.

## Reporting

Do not post a conduct report publicly if it identifies a target or exposes
private information. Use the repository's private process described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), prefix the report title with `Code of Conduct:`,
and include links or screenshots needed to understand the incident. For abuse
that occurs on GitHub, you may also use GitHub's platform reporting tools.

The maintainer will acknowledge a private report, preserve confidentiality as
far as practical, gather only the information needed to evaluate it, and tell
the reporter what action was taken when privacy permits.

## Enforcement

Responses are based on impact, context, and prior behavior. They may include a
private correction, removal of content, a temporary participation limit, or a
permanent ban. The maintainer will recuse when personally involved and seek a
neutral reviewer before deciding the outcome.

No contributor is exempt from this standard, including the project maintainer.
